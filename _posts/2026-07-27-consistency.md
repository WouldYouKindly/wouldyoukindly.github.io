It's (relatively) easy to be consistent with my routine when life is stable. As soon as I start travelling
though, or when there's a lot of social activity, the routine tends to fall apart, to certain extent. After
returning from a trip it takes about a week to bring the life to order. This is also when I tend to slip back
into bad habits.

At the same time I feel somewhat heroic when I succeed in keeping my routine during trips (brushing teeth,
skincare, exercise, taking vitamins.)
