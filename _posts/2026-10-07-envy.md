I'm riding my bike, and a car is parked by the side of the road. The road is narrow; the parked car effectively
turns it into a single-file. I get angry: it slows the buses, because they have to wait for other cars to pass.
And why? So that some guy could park his bloody car next to his house. Why should a bunch of people suffer
because of one person? (The car is on a parking spot - he doesn't break any rules, but the rules are dumb.)

The rules might be dumb, buy why am I getting so worked up about it? I'm on a bike, not on a bus. I'm on a bus
very rarely, actually. And I don't really like people, so who cares that they suffer? Why am I angry?

Well, it's the car, right? I hate cars. Cars are bad for the environment, and they require expensive infrastructure.
Why should I pay for the council to repair a road that these stupid cars batter every day? So that some stupid
guy can drive his Ferrari around and show everyone how cool he is? What a stupid fuck. And you know who's not stupid?
Me. I don't want a car.

But... what does owning a car mean? First, it means that you can drive. You're not scared; you probably can parallel
park. You are free to take your car and go to Lake District (while the guy on a bike has to take a train.) And then,
it  means you've got money - enough money to spend on a car, a luxury, entirely unnecessary in London. You don't give
a fuck. Who cares it's impractical? The important bit is that you can get into your car and drive your girlfriend to
Cornwall.

The car is a status symbol, so I want it. It doesn't matter whether I "rationally" think it's a good purchase. People
around me believe it's a status symbol, so I believe it too, and I want it. The same with the latest iphone. I know
it's dumb; still, deep down, I want it. I used to laugh at people who bought it because they though it made them cool.
Now I know that I am exactly the same as them. And the angry derision towards them stems from the fact that I cannot
afford it.

(The useful thought here might be that some of them cannot afford it too, they buy it on credit. But when you're
sorrounded by everyone with iphone 18 pro max++, your brain is panicking, thinking that you're rolling down to the
bottom of the social hierarchy, and there's not enough time to realise they're all actually idiots. There's only
enough time for a defense mechanism to activate.)
